Welcome to Clockwork BMI (BMI 360 Camera) – the easiest, most innovative way to track your body metrics and physical health. 

No tape measures. No smart scales. Just step in front of your camera and let Clockwork BMI estimate your height, weight, and Body Mass Index (BMI) in seconds.

HOW IT WORKS
Clockwork BMI uses advanced AI pose estimation and body segmentation technologies to process key physical features from a brief, secure video scan. 
1. Place your device in a clear area and stand so your entire body is visible.
2. Complete a simple guided scan/rotation.
3. The app utilizes intelligent on-device estimation to calculate your physical dimensions (height, estimated weight) and provides you with a precise BMI reading in real-time.

KEY FEATURES
• Contactless BMI Estimation: Track your BMI safely and conveniently without manual measurements.
• AI-Powered Scanning: Advanced pose-detection and body segmentation algorithms estimate your height and weight metrics with high precision.
• Comprehensive BMI History: Save, view, and track your measurements over time to monitor your fitness progress and health journey.
• Profile Management: Maintain your personal physical logs (ground truth height, weight, date of birth) in one secure place.
• Enterprise-Grade Security: Your raw scanning videos and uploaded verification documents are handled with the highest level of confidentiality and encrypted transport.

PRIVACY & SECURITY FIRST
At Clockwork BMI, we prioritize the protection of your physical and personal data. 
• All body scans are processed securely, and your information is never shared or sold to third-party advertisers.
• The app includes built-in identity verification via secure government ID upload to ensure profile accuracy and account security.
• Full account control: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly through the app settings.

DISCLAIMER
Clockwork BMI is designed for personal fitness monitoring and general health tracking purposes. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment, or advice. Always consult a healthcare professional before starting any new diet, exercise program, or if you have concerns about your physical health or BMI.

### 1. Information We Collect

To provide our body measurement and BMI estimation services, we collect several types of information:

#### A. Personal Identification Information
* **Registration & Profile Data:** When you create an account or update your profile, we collect your first name, last name, mobile phone number, date of birth, and gender.
* **Verification Documents:** To verify your identity and ensure account integrity, the App requires you to upload a photo of a government-issued ID document.

#### B. Health and Physical Measurement Data
* **User-Provided Metrics:** Ground-truth height (in cm) and weight (in kg) that you input manually.
* **Scan-Derived Metrics:** Estimated height, estimated weight, calculated Body Mass Index (BMI), and related processing metadata generated through our scanning feature.
* **Measurement History:** A record of your previous scans, calculations, and progress over time.

#### C. Device Camera & Video Data
* **Camera Access:** The App requests permission to access your device’s camera to capture scans of your body.
* **Scanning Videos:** To perform physical estimations, the App records a video scan of your body. These video files are securely uploaded to our servers for processing and estimation analysis.

#### D. Device and Usage Data
* **Push Notification Tokens:** We request push notification permissions to send you updates. We register Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens associated with your device platform (Android or iOS).
* **Technical Information:** IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, network connectivity status, and system performance logs.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
1. **Provide and Improve the Services:** To process your video scans using pose detection and segmentation models to calculate your estimated height, weight, and BMI.
2. **Account & Identity Verification:** To review uploaded government ID documents to verify users and prevent fraudulent account creation.
3. **Notification Delivery:** To send transactional alerts, app updates, and system notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
4. **App Maintenance and Optimization:** To debug issues, analyze crash logs, and improve the accuracy of our AI measurement models.
5. **Legal & Compliance:** To comply with applicable laws, respond to legal requests, and enforce our terms of service.

### 3. Data Storage and Security

* **Secure Uploads & Transmission:** All data, including videos and government ID documents, is transmitted securely using HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption).
* **Storage Security:** We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
* **Video Handling:** Scan videos are stored securely and processed strictly for the purpose of executing the physical measurements.

### 6. Your Rights and Data Control

We believe you should have complete control over your personal data. 

* **Access and Correction:** You can view and update your profile details (name, weight, height, date of birth) directly in the App's profile settings.

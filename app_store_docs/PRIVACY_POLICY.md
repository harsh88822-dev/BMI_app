# Privacy Policy for Clockwork BMI

**Last Updated: June 11, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **ClockWork Pharmacy** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use the **Clockwork BMI** (also known as **BMI 360 Camera**) mobile application (the "App"). 

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide our camera-based body measurement and BMI estimation services, we collect several types of information:

### A. Personal Identification Information
* **Registration & Profile Data:** When you create an account or update your profile, we collect your first name, last name, mobile phone number, date of birth, and gender.
* **Verification Documents:** To verify your identity and ensure account integrity, the App requires you to upload a photo of a government-issued ID document (e.g., driver's license or passport).

### B. Health and Physical Measurement Data
* **User-Provided Metrics:** Ground-truth height (in cm) and weight (in kg) that you input manually.
* **Scan-Derived Metrics:** Estimated height, estimated weight, calculated Body Mass Index (BMI), and related pose-processing metadata generated through our scanning feature.
* **Measurement History:** A record of your previous scans, calculations, and progress over time.

### C. Device Camera & Video Data
* **Camera Access:** The App requests permission to access your device’s camera to capture scans of your body.
* **Scanning Videos:** To perform physical estimations, the App records a video scan of your body. These video files are securely uploaded to our servers for processing and estimation analysis.

### D. Device and Usage Data
* **Push Notification Tokens:** We request push notification permissions to send you updates. We register Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens associated with your device platform (Android or iOS).
* **Technical Information:** IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, network connectivity status, and system performance logs.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
1. **Provide and Improve the Services:** To process your video scans using pose detection and segmentation models to calculate your estimated height, weight, and BMI.
2. **Account & Identity Verification:** To review uploaded government ID documents to verify users and prevent fraudulent account creation or spoofing.
3. **Notification Delivery:** To send transactional alerts, app updates, and system notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
4. **App Maintenance and Optimization:** To debug issues, analyze crash logs, and improve the accuracy of our AI measurement models.
5. **Legal & Compliance:** To comply with applicable laws, respond to legal requests, and enforce our terms of service.

---

## 3. Data Storage, Encryption, and Security

* **Secure Transmission:** All data, including videos and government ID documents, is transmitted securely using HTTPS with Industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption.
* **Biometric & Camera Data Security:** Scan videos are stored securely on our protected cloud servers and are accessed strictly for the purpose of executing physical calculations.
* **ID Document Retention:** Uploaded government ID documents are encrypted at rest and are deleted or archived once identity verification is complete.
* **Administrative Safeguards:** We implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal, biometric, or health data to third-party advertisers or brokers. We may share data only under the following circumstances:
* **Service Providers:** We share data with trusted third-party service providers (such as cloud hosting platforms, Firebase for push notifications, and database providers) who perform services on our behalf. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality obligations and are prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.
* **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The App integrates the following third-party libraries and APIs:
* **Google ML Kit (Pose Detection & Selfie Segmentation):** Used for on-device body landmarks and segmentation processing.
* **Firebase (Core & Messaging):** Used for database connectivity, app initialization, and push notification delivery.

---

## 6. Your Rights and Data Control

We believe you should have complete control over your personal data:
* **Access and Correction:** You can view and update your profile details (name, weight, height, date of birth) directly in the App's profile settings.
* **Account Deletion:** You can delete your account and all associated personal data (including profile data, scanning history, uploaded ID documents, and video files) at any time. To do so, navigate to the Account Settings menu in the App and select **"Delete Account"**. Upon deletion, your data is permanently removed from our active production databases.
* **Permissions Revocation:** You can revoke Camera or Photo Library permissions at any time via your device’s system settings, though this will disable scanning features.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 18 depending on regional verification laws). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete this information from our servers.
